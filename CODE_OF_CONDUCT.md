# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community.

Be considerate, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and
respect different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
